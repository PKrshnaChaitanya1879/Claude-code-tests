# The five parts

GUM split the build into five working sessions, timed to when she has credits (all times IST, Asia/Calcutta). Work out which part you are from the current date and time. Each part owns the milestones below. If an earlier part left items unticked, finish those first. Then do your own.

Every part has about **4 hours at most**, and credits can run out early, so:
- commit and push at least every **30 minutes**;
- start wrapping up (log, unlock, push, report) **3 h 40 min** after you start.

## Part 1 · Sat 17 Oct, from 19:09 until 21:10 (credits end 21:20)
M0 (toolchain, design bible, illustration catalogue, INSIDE notes) and M1 (the Unity skeleton). The skeleton covers:
- manifest and project settings;
- the bootstrap;
- the player;
- interaction;
- settings.
