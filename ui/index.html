How Grvty works

  1. Add photos. Drop RAW or JPEG files anywhere in this window.
  2. Describe a look or tap an idea, and choose how many versions to see.
  3. Pick your favourite. Ask for more like it, fine-tune it, or save it as a Lightroom preset.
  4. Export one photo or a whole batch with the same look.
